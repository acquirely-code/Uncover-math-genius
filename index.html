What Makes UMG Different

Uncover Math Genius is not your typical Math class or a formula-drilling center.

We are a learning movement that helps children fall in love with Math, by uncovering the genius within them — one moment of clarity, joy, and real-world connection at a time.

1. Personalized Learning Paths

Every child learns differently. That’s why we place them on a learning path — not just a grade level. 

Our 4 signature learning journeys are based on cognitive readiness, not just syllabus deadlines.

2. Research-Backed Frameworks

Each learning path is guided by a unique, age-appropriate framework — from Math Over Moments (MoM) in early grades to SCOPE for senior secondary. These frameworks are rooted in modern pedagogies like Inquiry-Based Math, and Visible Learning.

3. Expert-Led, Coach-Supported

Your child learns from Math coaches trained by Dr. Garima herself on deep subject knowledge with emotional intelligence and child psychology. Think of them as Math Mentors — not just Math teachers.

4. Real-World Relevance

From grocery store Math to geometry in nature, we connect abstract concepts to real life — making Math feel useful, visual, and fun.

5. Conceptual Clarity Before Speed

We prioritize deep understanding before pushing for speed or marks. This helps reduce Math anxiety and builds long-term confidence.

6. Visual Tools, Stories & Games

Every lesson includes visual models, relatable stories, puzzles, or strategy games — making Math engaging and memorable.

7. Regular Parent Partnerships

You’re part of the journey too. We include parents through monthly check-ins, progress reports, and tools to support your child at home.

The UMG Learning Paths

Framework: MoM – Math Over Moments

Where playful curiosity sparks early numeracy and problem-solving love.

“With wide eyes and a world to explore,
They chase numbers like stars on the floor.
Each sum a secret, each shape a clue —
Math begins as a journey new.”

Here, Math is not taught — it’s discovered. Children learn by playing, exploring, and asking questions. We build confidence through stories, puzzles, games, and everyday Math.

We nurture the attitude:

“I wonder why… What happens if… Can I try it this way?”

Focus Areas:

Foundational concepts in numbers, shapes, and measurements

Building estimation and reasoning skills

Visual models & storytelling in problem-solving

Cultivating a growth mindset

Perfect for children who are still developing confidence and love asking questions.

Framework: MAP – Math as a Process

Builds logical reasoning, structured thinking, and joyful math habits.

“Blocks of logic, bricks of thought,
Every why and how is sought.
They test, they build, they break, they mend —
Where reasoning and rules become friends.”

This is where children start connecting the why and how of Math. We guide them to break problems down, reason step-by-step, and find joy in logical construction.

We nurture the attitude:

“Why does this method work? Can I find another way?”

Focus Areas:

Algebraic thinking, fractions, ratios, geometry basics

Systematic problem-solving approaches

Interpreting word problems

Introduction to real-world applications

Ideal for children who are curious but need a scaffolded, logical pathway.

Framework: IDEA – Inquiry-Driven Exploration in Abstractions

Supports deep thinking, abstraction, and conceptual fluency.

“No longer just answers, now meanings unfold,
In symbols and systems, the abstract takes hold.
They twist and turn in mental flight — Solving puzzles hidden from sight.”

This stage invites students to think deeply. Math becomes a landscape of abstract connections — where we focus on understanding before speed, and why before how.

We nurture the attitude:

“This feels challenging… but I want to understand it deeply.”

Focus Areas:

Algebra, geometry, trigonometry, probability

Conceptual understanding before formula memorization

Inquiry-based projects

Preparation for board exam clarity + real-world relevance

Great for students who are capable but often overwhelmed, or those needing more clarity before speed.

Framework: SCOPE – Strategic Concepts & Problem-solving Essentials

Prepares learners for competitive exams, careers, and real-world applications.

“They calculate futures, forecast with flair,
Their thinking is sharp, precise, and rare.
Equations serve purpose, data reveals truth —
Strategy meets wisdom in this fearless youth.”

At this level, we combine depth with direction. Students are guided to master core concepts strategically, while building confidence for boards, entrances, and future careers.

We nurture the attitude:

“I know I need to do well — but I also want to understand it for life.”

Focus Areas:

Advanced Algebra, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry

Structured exam strategies

Data analysis, optimization problems

Career-linked Math relevance: Engineering, Economics, Design, etc.

Best for students with college goals or those preparing for competitive exams.

The UMG Pedagogy:

Designed for the Thinkers of Tomorrow  

We don’t just teach Math. We build confident problem-solvers, creative thinkers, and future-ready minds.  

At Uncover Math Genius, we believe:

Math is not about memorizing formulas — it’s about understanding how the world works.

Math isn’t taught — it’s uncovered, explored, and experienced.

And no child should feel they’re “just not a Math person.”

That’s why every UMG session is built on research-backed, child-centered pedagogy — where students ask questions, explore ideas, build connections, and learn how to think.

Why We Teach in Small Groups ?

Because real learning doesn’t happen in isolation.

It happens in interaction.

In our carefully designed group sessions (4–8 students), something powerful happens:

Children stop being passive listeners.
They start becoming curious thinkers.
They explain. They question. They debate. They discover.
More Thinking, Less Watching

In 1:1 classes, a child often waits for direction.

In small groups, they process independently, hear other perspectives, and actively participate — which cements learning.

Learning Becomes Collaborative

Children hear how peers solve problems differently.

This peer exposure builds flexibility, logic, and openness to new strategies.
Safe Space for Questions

Not all kids are confident in 1:1 settings.

In a small group, they see peers ask too — and feel safe to voice their doubts without fear of judgment.

Real-Life Application

We rarely solve problems alone in real life.

Our group format mirrors how teams work — discuss, analyze, solve — building skills beyond academics.

It’s deeply effective — because the world your child is growing into needs collaboration, communication, and critical thinking just as much as correct answers.

What You Can Expect:

Sessions that are interactive, visual, and discovery-based

Real-world anchoring, not just textbook completion

Peer discussions that unlock new perspectives

Coaches who see your child as more than just a score
